2️⃣ Protocols & Guides — circRNA Knockdown (HIGH PRIORITY)

Title:
Guide to circRNA Knockdown Using Junction-Specific shRNAs

Description (use exactly this text):
A detailed experimental and conceptual guide for designing, validating, and using short hairpin RNAs (shRNAs) to specifically knock down circular RNAs in vivo without affecting their linear host transcripts. This approach, developed in the Kadener Lab, targets the unique back-splice junction of circRNAs and has enabled functional studies of circRNAs in Drosophila development, neural function, aging, and disease models.
